## 🔧 Step-by-Step Setup Guide

### Step 1: Prepare Your Quest Headset

1. Put on your Quest headset and go to the **Settings** menu
2. Navigate to **System** → **Developer**
3. Turn on **Developer Mode** (you'll need a Meta developer account - it's free to create)
4. Connect your headset to your PC using the USB cable
5. When prompted, **allow USB debugging** on your headset

### Step 2: Set Up Half-Life 2 on Steam

1. Open Steam on your PC and go to your **Library**
2. Right-click on **Half-Life 2** and select **Properties**
3. Click on the **Betas** tab
4. From the dropdown menu, select **`steam_legacy`**
5. Wait for the game to update (this may take a few minutes)
6. Verify the update finishes successfully - this is the only version SourceVR is tested with

### Step 5: Import Your Game Files

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "My game files won't import"
Make sure you've switched to the **`steam_legacy`** branch in Steam BEFORE trying to import. The importer checks for this specific version.

### "I see an option to launch a different build"
The importer will sometimes offer to use a different build if your files pass other checks but don't match the exact version. This is untested and at your own risk.

### "The game won't install"
Double-check that Developer Mode is enabled on your headset and that USB debugging is allowed.

### "I have a different version of Half-Life 2"
Only the `steam_legacy` branch with PatchVersion `8491853` is officially supported. Other versions may work but are untested.
